# Email Style Guide

## My Name / Signature

Petra Toth

## Structure

- Start with a greeting
- Acknowledge what the message is about
- End with a next step or action item
- Sign off with "Best regards," followed by my name

## Tone

- Match the sender's tone (if it's a reply)
- Keep it natural (should not sound as generic AI)
- Use the recipient's first name
- Keep paragraphs short (max. 5 sentesces)
- Use "Have a nice day!" before 6:00 PM and "Have a nice evening!" after 6:00 PM

## Rules

- Always proofread for typos
- If declining something, offer an alternative
- Do not use emojis or gifs
- Do not use em dash (—)
